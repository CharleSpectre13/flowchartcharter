# Loop Engineering Contract — FlowChartCharter Continuous Learning

## Goals
Replace repetitive human prompting during FlowChartCharter engineering with verified, budgeted, isolated loops that raise quality every cycle.

## House Rules
1. Maker-checker separation mandatory.
2. continuous-team-audit-loop after every meaningful step and before every public repo creation.
3. Falsifiable stop conditions; no infinite runs.
4. External memory: decisions-log, known-failures, past-runs.
5. Charter remains primary; GraphRAG only as tool.

## Stop Conditions
Stop when: (a) all TASKS.md verification criteria V1–V8 pass AND (b) latest Cross-Reference Report has zero blocking issues OR max_turns=20 OR consecutive_failures=3 OR cost_exceeds budget.

## Tone
Direct, evidence-based, zero empty motivational filler.
